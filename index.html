OrbitFiles

Move files fast between your phone and cloud storage, or from cloud to cloud.

  • Copy and move photos, videos and documents from your Android phone to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Mega, Backblaze B2, Amazon S3 and S3-compatible storage, or your own SFTP, FTP and WebDAV servers.
  • Copy straight from one cloud to another, for example from Google Drive to S3.
  • Browse, open, rename and delete files on any of them, and bring files back to your phone.
  • Transfers keep going in the background, wait out dropped connections, and every copy is checked once it lands.

Private by design

No account and no sign-up. When you connect a service, you sign in on that service's own page, and the sign-in is kept only on your phone, in Android's encrypted storage. Your files travel directly between your phone and the services you choose: we run no server and never see them. The free version shows ads from Google; your files and connections are never part of them.

Get it

OrbitFiles for Android is in testing on Google Play and coming soon to everyone.
