OrbitFiles privacy policy and terms

Last updated: 5 November 2026

OrbitFiles is a file manager for Android that moves files between your phone and the cloud storage and servers you connect to it. It is made by Usman Bhana ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we don't collect any of it ourselves. The free version shows ads from Google, which uses some device data to do so (see "Ads" below).

What we collect

Nothing. OrbitFiles has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. We run no server that the app talks to, so your files, file names, connection details and usage never reach us. The only third party built into the app is Google's ad service, described below.

What stays on your phone

  • Connection details. When you connect a service (for example Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, pCloud, Box, S3, SFTP, FTP or WebDAV), the sign-in tokens, passwords and keys are saved only on your phone, in Android's encrypted secure storage. They are used only to talk to that service.
  • App data. Your settings, recent folders and transfer history are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of this.

Where your files go

Files go only where you send them: from your phone to a service you connected, from that service to your phone, or between two services you connected. They travel directly between your phone and those services over encrypted connections where the service supports them (FTP and plain WebDAV servers may not). What each service does with your files is covered by its own privacy policy.

When you sign in to a service, its own sign-in page opens in your browser. OrbitFiles receives only the access token the service hands back.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, OrbitFiles asks Google for access to your Drive (the drive scope). The app uses it only to do what you ask on screen: list your folders and files, open, download, upload, copy, move, rename and delete them, and show how much space is left.

  • The access token Google returns is stored only on your phone, in Android's encrypted secure storage. Removing the Google Drive connection in the app, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. You can also remove OrbitFiles' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your Drive files, file names and folder contents go only between your phone, Google Drive and the other services you choose. They never reach us: we have no server.
  • Google Drive data is never used for advertising, never shared with Google's ad service or anyone else, never sold, and never used to train AI models. No person reads it.

OrbitFiles' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Ads

The free version of OrbitFiles shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, and sometimes a full-screen ad when you open Transfers after a transfer has finished. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (to estimate your general location), basic device information and how you interact with the ads. Google doesn't get your files, file names or connections. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in More › Privacy choices for ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy › Ads).
  • Subscribers to OrbitFiles Pro, once it is available, see no ads.

Permissions

  • All files access and photos, videos and audio: to show the files on your phone and copy them to and from your connected services.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show the progress of transfers and keep them going when the app is in the background.
  • Internet and network state: to reach the services you connect, to wait for Wi-Fi if you choose "Transfer on Wi-Fi only", and to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ad service, as described under "Ads".

Sharing

We don't sell, share or transfer any information to anyone, because we don't have any. The ad data above goes from the app directly to Google, under Google's policies.

Children

OrbitFiles is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Terms of use

  • You're responsible for the services you connect and for following their terms. OrbitFiles only acts on your instructions.
  • Moving files deletes the originals after the copy has been checked, and deleting files may be permanent on some services. Keep backups of anything important; we aren't liable for lost or damaged files, to the extent the law allows.
  • Nothing here affects your rights under the law where you live.
